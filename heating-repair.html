Repair-first heating diagnostics

When your heating system stops keeping your home comfortable, the cause may be a control, airflow, ignition or mechanical problem. Comfort Dynamics focuses on diagnosing the problem before recommending replacement.

Heating systems we service

  • Furnaces — no heat, ignition problems, short cycling and weak airflow.
  • Boilers and hydronic heating — circulation, zone controls and uneven heat.
  • Heat pumps — heating performance, defrost and auxiliary heat concerns.
  • Garage and shop heaters — starting and heating problems.

What to expect

We discuss your symptoms, diagnose the equipment and explain the findings before work is done. If repair is practical, that is our first approach.
