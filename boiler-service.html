Boiler Problems We Diagnose

  • Boiler will not fire or has no heat
  • Some zones heat while others stay cold
  • Radiators or baseboards are not heating properly
  • Radiant-floor loops are not heating evenly
  • Boiler is leaking or losing pressure
  • Banging, gurgling, or unusual system noise
  • Short cycling or frequent burner operation
  • Control, thermostat, or zone problems

More Than Just the Boiler

Hydronic heating problems can come from the boiler itself or from the components moving and controlling the water. We look at the complete system, including circulator pumps, zone valves, expansion tanks, air elimination, pressure, thermostats and controls, and accessible piping issues.

Radiant Floor & Combi Boiler Service

We also work with hydronic systems that use radiant-floor heat and combination boilers. These systems can have different symptoms than a standard furnace, so understanding flow, temperature, zoning, and controls matters when diagnosing them.

Repair-first boiler service: the goal is to identify the actual problem and repair what makes sense before discussing replacement.

Boiler Service FAQs

Do you work on radiant-floor heating?

Yes. We diagnose hydronic radiant-floor heating problems, including flow, zoning, controls, and boiler-related issues.

Why is only one boiler zone cold?

A cold zone can be related to a circulator, zone valve, thermostat, control, air in the system, or a flow problem. The correct fix depends on what testing shows.

Do you work on combi boilers?

Yes. We service combination boiler systems and troubleshoot heating-side operating problems and related controls.

Does a noisy boiler need replacement?

Not necessarily. Noise can come from several repairable conditions. We diagnose the source before recommending major work.
