Privacy Policy

How Comfort Dynamics Heating & Cooling Specialists handles information you provide when you contact us or request service.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Information We Collect

When you contact us, request service, or submit a lead form, we may receive information such as your name, phone number, email address, ZIP code or service address, and details about the heating or cooling service you need. If you contact us by phone or email, we receive the information you choose to provide.

How We Use Information

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, determine whether your property is in our service area, discuss availability and pricing, schedule or provide service, and maintain ordinary business records.

Who May Process Information

Lead forms may be provided by Meta. We may use service providers that help operate our website, receive or manage inquiries, or communicate with customers. Those providers process information as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect our rights and the safety of others.

Website Storage and Analytics

This website uses browser local storage to keep a limited history of page views and clicks on phone links on your device. The current site script stores this information in your browser and does not send it to Comfort Dynamics. Your browser settings let you clear local storage. If we add analytics or other tracking tools that collect or transmit website activity, we will update this policy to describe them.

How Long We Keep Information

We keep inquiry and service information for as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide service, maintain business records, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Your Choices and Questions

You can choose what information to provide. If you want to ask about, correct, or request deletion of information you submitted, contact us using the details below. We may need to retain some information for completed work or required business records.
