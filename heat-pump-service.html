Heat pumps in Fargo-Moorhead operate through a much wider temperature range than systems in mild climates. Cold-weather performance depends on the outdoor unit, indoor airflow, defrost operation, controls, backup heat when applicable, and the condition of the refrigeration system.

Inverter & Variable-Speed Heat Pumps

Modern inverter-driven heat pumps can modulate instead of simply switching on and off. Diagnosing them may involve checking operating data, sensors, controls, airflow, outdoor-unit condition, and system performance rather than replacing a part based on one symptom.

Heat Pump FAQs

Do heat pumps work in Fargo-Moorhead winters?

Cold-climate heat pumps are designed to operate at low outdoor temperatures, but actual performance depends on the equipment, installation, controls, building load, and backup-heat setup.

Why is my heat pump covered in frost?

Some frost can occur during heating operation and should be handled by the defrost cycle. Heavy or persistent icing can point to a defrost, airflow, control, or refrigeration-system problem.

Why is my heat pump running constantly?

Long run times can be normal in some conditions, especially with variable-speed equipment, but poor airflow, control problems, low capacity, or system performance issues can also cause excessive operation.

Do you work on inverter heat pumps?

Yes. We diagnose modern inverter and variable-speed heat pump systems as well as conventional heat pumps.
