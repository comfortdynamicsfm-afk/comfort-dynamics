No Heat? Start With a Real Diagnosis

When a furnace quits in a Fargo-Moorhead winter, the goal is simple: find the failure, explain it clearly, and get the heat back on when a sensible repair is available. We do not start with a replacement pitch.

Furnace Problems We Diagnose

  • Furnace will not start
  • Furnace blows cold or lukewarm air
  • Ignition or burner problems
  • Short cycling or repeated lockouts
  • Weak airflow or cold rooms
  • Unusual furnace noises
  • Thermostat or control problems
  • Safety or venting concerns

Common Furnace Repairs

Depending on the diagnosis, furnace problems can involve components such as igniters, flame sensors, pressure switches, inducer motors, blower motors, capacitors, thermostats, controls, airflow restrictions, or venting issues. We test the system before recommending parts.

Repair-first service: if the furnace can be repaired safely and sensibly, repair is the first option. If replacement truly makes more sense, we will explain why.

Furnace Safety & Combustion

Some heating problems are not just comfort issues. Burner operation, venting, pressure-switch operation, flame quality, and other safety-related conditions can affect how a furnace operates. When appropriate, we use testing and operating measurements to help identify the problem.

Furnace Repair FAQs

Do you offer same-day furnace repair?

Same-day service may be available depending on the schedule, location, weather, and parts availability. Calling early gives you the best chance of getting on the schedule.

Why is my furnace running but not heating?

There are several possible causes, including ignition problems, burner issues, airflow problems, control faults, or a system lockout. Testing the furnace is the best way to determine the actual cause.

Will you repair an older furnace?

Yes, when the furnace can be repaired safely and the repair makes sense. Age alone does not automatically mean the furnace needs replacement.

Do you service furnaces before winter?

Yes. If the furnace is operating and you want preventive service rather than a repair visit, our furnace tune-up includes combustion analysis and safety testing.
